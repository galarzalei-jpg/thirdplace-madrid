FAQ

Before you join

Is there a joining fee?

No. You only pay the monthly fee of your level. No referrals, no interviews.

Can I cancel or change my level?

Yes, any time from the members area. Upgrades apply straight away; cancellations at the end of the month you've paid.

Will I always get in?

On weekdays almost always. On Friday and Saturday nights space is limited, so Social members enter while there's room and Plus and Elite members can reserve their spot in advance.

Can I bring friends who aren't members?

Yes. Any member can buy a €15 guest day pass. Plus includes 2 free guests a month and Elite includes 4.

Do I have to drink alcohol?

Not at all. You pay a fixed fee, so we win when you stay, not when you drink. We have a full mocktail menu and coffee.

How old do I need to be?

18 or over. Most members are between 22 and 35.

Are food and drinks included?

Members get discounts on everything. Elite members also get a monthly food and drink credit and a welcome drink on every visit.
