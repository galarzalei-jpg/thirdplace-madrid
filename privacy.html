This website is a school project. Everything you enter (account, bookings, cart, messages) is saved only in your own browser and is never sent to us or anyone else. This policy shows how the club would handle data once it opens.

ThirdPlace Madrid, Calle de Fernando VI, 3, 28004 Madrid, is the data controller. For anything about your data, write to privacidad@thirdplacemadrid.com.

3. How long we keep it

  • Account and booking data: while you are a member, then 12 months to deal with any queries.
  • Billing records: 6 years, as Spanish tax law requires.
  • Contact messages: 12 months. Waiting-list email: until you unsubscribe.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. We share only what is needed with providers who work for us: our payment provider, our email service and our website host (GitHub Pages). Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address to deliver them. Where a provider is outside the EU, it is covered by an EU adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses.

5. Your rights

Under the GDPR and Spain's data protection law (LOPDGDD) you can, at any time:

  • access your data and get a copy of it;
  • correct it, or ask us to delete it;
  • limit or object to how we use it;
  • take it to another provider (portability);
  • withdraw your consent, without affecting what we did before.

Write to privacidad@thirdplacemadrid.com and we will answer within one month. If you are not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).

6. Security and minors

We use HTTPS and limit access to personal data to the staff who need it. The club is for adults only, so we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.
