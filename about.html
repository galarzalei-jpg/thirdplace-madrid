Our story

Why "the third place"?

Home is your first place. Work or uni is your second. The third is where friendships happen.

The name comes from the American sociologist Ray Oldenburg and his book The Great Good Place (1989). He described the "third place": the café, the neighbourhood bar or the barbershop where you know everyone's name, and where communities are born.

Many cities have lost those places. Young people meet at home, if the flat is big enough, or in venues built for them to spend and leave. ThirdPlace brings the third place back, the way people in their 20s and 30s actually live it: with sofas, games, good food and a room of your own for the night.

That's also why our symbol is a simple 3. Home, work, and then us.
